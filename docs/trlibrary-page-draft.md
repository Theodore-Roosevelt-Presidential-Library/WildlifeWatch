**Wildlife of the Badlands**

## Intro

When T.R. stepped off the train in the Dakota Badlands in September 1883, he came to hunt bison while a few still remained. Within his lifetime the great herds were gone, along with the region's elk and bighorn sheep. All three roam here again because people decided they should — the same conviction that shaped Roosevelt's conservation record as President.

Theodore Roosevelt National Park begins at the edge of Medora, minutes from the Library. Start the day at the Library, then head into the park at first light — or reverse it.
